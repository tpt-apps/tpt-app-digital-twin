## Product Description (ready to paste)

TPT Digital Twin Workbench is a unified desktop environment for building and iterating on physical digital twins.

It combines:

- Parametric 3D CAD with real-time multi-user editing
- Git-like versioning of geometry and parameters
- Local physics and process simulation
- Sensor fusion visualisation
- A single local database for the entire twin

Everything runs on your machines. No cloud account, no telemetry, no subscription.

You receive pre-built native binaries for Windows, macOS and Linux together with a license key file.

This is a one-time purchase. No updates are promised. You may submit approved error reports that may influence a future major release.
